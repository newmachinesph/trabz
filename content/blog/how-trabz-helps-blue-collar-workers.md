# How Trabz is Revolutionizing Blue-Collar Employment in the Philippines

The blue-collar job market in the Philippines faces unique challenges: workers struggle to find quality opportunities, while employers spend weeks searching for reliable talent. **Trabz** is changing that.

## The Problem We're Solving

### For Workers:
- **Limited access** to job opportunities beyond their network
- **Lack of trust** in online job platforms
- **No skill verification** to stand out from other applicants
- **Delayed payments** and unfair wage practices

### For Employers:
- **Time-consuming** hiring process (average 45 days to fill a position)
- **High turnover** rates due to poor job matching
- **Difficulty verifying** worker skills and reliability
- **No data** on worker performance and attendance

## Our Solution: A Complete Employment Ecosystem

### 1. **Smart Job Matching**

Our AI-powered matching algorithm connects workers with jobs that fit their:
- Skills and experience
- Location preferences
- Salary expectations
- Work schedule needs

Workers get personalized job recommendations, while employers receive pre-screened candidates.

### 2. **Trust Through Verification**

Every worker builds a verified profile with:
- **KayaScore** - Psychometric assessment measuring work ethic, reliability, and teamwork
- **Performance ratings** from previous employers
- **Skill certifications** from TESDA-aligned courses
- **Attendance history** and completion rates

### 3. **Fast & Flexible Work Options**

Beyond traditional full-time jobs, Trabz offers:
- **Gig marketplace** - Same-day work opportunities
- **Shift work** - Morning, afternoon, or night shifts
- **Temp-to-hire** - Try before committing
- **Instant pay** - Get paid on the same day

### 4. **Financial Inclusion**

Our integrated **e-wallet** provides:
- Earned Wage Access (withdraw up to 50% of earned wages)
- Fee-free digital payments
- Remittance services for OFWs
- Micro-loans for workers

### 5. **Continuous Learning**

Workers can **upskill** through:
- Free TESDA-aligned courses
- Offline-first learning (works without internet)
- Digital certifications
- Career pathways guidance
