# KayaScore: The Complete Guide to Psychometric Assessments for Workers

Finding the right job match goes beyond skills and experience. **KayaScore** is Trabz's proprietary psychometric assessment that helps workers showcase their strengths and helps employers identify candidates who will thrive in their workplace.

## What is KayaScore?

KayaScore is a 20-minute online assessment that measures five critical workplace traits:

### 1. **Work Ethic** (Sipag)
- Dedication and commitment to tasks
- Willingness to go the extra mile
- Consistency in effort

### 2. **Teamwork** (Pakikisama)
- Ability to collaborate with others
- Communication skills
- Conflict resolution

### 3. **Stress Tolerance** (Kaya)
- How you handle pressure and deadlines
- Emotional stability
- Adaptability to change

### 4. **Integrity** (Tapat)
- Honesty and trustworthiness
- Adherence to rules and ethics
- Reliability

### 5. **Adaptability** (Flexible)
- Openness to learning
- Response to new situations
- Problem-solving approach

## How Does It Work?

### Step 1: Complete the Assessment (20 minutes)
- 45 scenario-based questions
- No right or wrong answers
- Answer honestly for best results
- Can be done in Filipino or English

### Step 2: Receive Your Score
You'll get scores in each of the 5 domains:
- **0-40**: Needs Development
- **41-60**: Average
- **61-80**: Above Average
- **81-100**: Exceptional

### Step 3: Share with Employers
Your KayaScore badge appears on your Trabz profile, helping you stand out to employers.

## Sample Scenarios

Here's an example of what a KayaScore question looks like:

> **Scenario:** Your supervisor asks you to finish an urgent task before leaving, but you have plans with family tonight. What do you do?
>
> A) Complete the task and call your family to reschedule
> B) Tell your supervisor you can't stay tonight
> C) Offer to come in early tomorrow to finish
> D) Ask a teammate to help so you can leave on time

There are no "wrong" answers - we're measuring how you approach work-life situations.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I retake the assessment?**
Yes, but you must wait 30 days between attempts to ensure score validity.

**How long is my score valid?**
Depends on the tier you purchased (7, 30, or 60 days). After expiration, you'll need to retake it.

**Will employers see my low scores?**
You control which scores to share. You can hide your KayaScore if you're not satisfied with the results.

**Is KayaScore required?**
No, but 73% of employers filter for candidates with verified assessments.

**What if I disagree with my results?**
We offer a free consultation with our career coaches to help you understand your score and create an improvement plan.
