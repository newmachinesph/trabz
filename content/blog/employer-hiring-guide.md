# Employer's Guide: How to Hire Quality Blue-Collar Workers in 24 Hours

Hiring blue-collar workers in the Philippines doesn't have to take weeks or cost thousands of pesos. With **Trabz**, you can find qualified, verified candidates in as little as 24 hours. Here's how.

## The Traditional Hiring Problem

Most Philippine employers face these challenges:

- **45-60 days** average time-to-hire
- **₱15,000-30,000** cost per hire (agency fees)
- **60-70%** turnover rate in first 6 months
- **Limited candidate pool** from newspaper ads or walk-ins
- **No verification** of skills or work history
- **Time wasted** on no-shows and unqualified applicants

## The Trabz Solution: 5-Step Hiring Process

### Step 1: Post Your Job (5 minutes)

Create a job posting with:
- **Job title** and category
- **Location** (specific or general area)
- **Salary range** (daily or monthly)
- **Requirements** (education, experience, skills)
- **Job type** (full-time, part-time, contract, gig)

**Pro Tips:**
- ✅ Be specific about daily responsibilities
- ✅ Mention perks (SSS, PhilHealth, free meals, transport allowance)
- ✅ Add "instant pay" or "temp-to-hire" if applicable
- ✅ Use clear Filipino or English (or both)

### Step 2: Enable KayaScore Filter (Optional)

Require candidates to have:
- Minimum KayaScore (e.g., 60+)
- Specific domains (e.g., Work Ethic 70+, Teamwork 65+)
- Dark trait screening (avoid problematic hires)

**Result:** 60% fewer applications, but 3x higher quality

### Step 3: Review Applicants (Same Day)

You'll start receiving applications within hours. Each profile shows:

- **Complete work history** with ratings
- **Skills** and certifications
- **KayaScore** (if required)
- **Attendance rate** and completion rate
- **Reviews** from previous employers
- **Location** and availability

**Filter by:**
- Distance from work location
- Minimum rating (4+ stars)
- Experience level
- Availability (immediate start vs. 2-week notice)

### Step 4: Message & Interview (1-2 days)

Use Trabz's built-in messaging to:
- Request additional info
- Schedule phone or in-person interviews
- Send pre-screening questions
- Share company details

**Time-Saving Tip:** Send the same message to your top 5 candidates simultaneously.

### Step 5: Hire (Same Day)

Once you select a candidate:
1. Click "Hire" button
2. Set start date and onboarding details
3. Send contract (optional)
4. Track attendance and performance

**You only pay ₱500 when you successfully hire.**

## Best Practices for Hiring on Trabz

### 1. Write Clear Job Descriptions

**Bad Example:**
> "Helper needed, ₱500/day, Makati area"

**Good Example:**
> **Warehouse Helper - Makati**
> ₱650/day + OT pay + SSS benefits
>
> **Responsibilities:**
> - Load/unload delivery trucks
> - Organize inventory
> - Basic cleaning
>
> **Requirements:**
> - 18-40 years old
> - Can lift 25kg
> - Willing to work weekends
> - Preferably with warehouse experience
>
> **Benefits:**
> - Free lunch
> - 13th month pay
> - Performance bonus
> - Temp-to-hire with ₱100/day increase after 3 months

### 2. Respond Quickly

- Reply to applications within 24 hours
- 73% of candidates apply to multiple jobs
- First to respond gets first choice of best candidates

### 3. Use Video Screening (Optional)

- Request 30-second video introduction
- Reduces no-shows by 45%
- Assess communication skills
- Verify candidate seriousness

### 4. Set Clear Expectations

During interviews, discuss:
- Exact working hours and schedule
- Salary breakdown (basic + allowances + deductions)
- Performance expectations
- Growth opportunities
- Company rules and culture

### 5. Give Feedback

After interviews:
- Update application status in the system
- Send rejection messages (good candidates might fit future roles)
- Rate workers you hired (helps other employers)
